# The lead decides retro action items, with named exceptions

## Rule

The Technical Lead approves, skips or defers each retro action item and records it in the learnings file. Items that add a step for the human, change product behaviour or customer-facing copy, or cost money go to the human.

## Why

Routing every item to the human slowed retros; with the lead deciding, all items in a window were decided at their retro.

## How to check

Every action item row has a status and who decided it.
