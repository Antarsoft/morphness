# Legal briefs name the source of each personal value

## Rule

A brief to the legal writer names where each personal value in the copy comes from (whose email a form sends, which account a record belongs to).

## Why

A legal draft described the wrong person's email because the brief didn't say whose it was.

## How to check

Each personal value in the brief has a source line.
