## What changed

`harness/MORPHING.md` now says that an agent without the plugin can follow `DOCTOR.md`, `MODULE.md` and `SHARE.md` in the Morphness repository, as it already could follow `UPDATE.md`.

## Why

In a Claude Projects thread `/plugin` can't be typed, so `/morph-share` and the other slash commands had nothing to run.

## How to apply

Merge the listed file. Nothing else to change.
