## What changed

A fourth mode next to full, light and research: **bug mode**, for something that worked (or that an
AC or the docs promise) and is broken. The Technical Lead writes a short bug plan from
`harness/templates/bug-plan.md` (symptom, where seen, expected and its source, reproduction, the code
path it read, the regression test, a fix limit, the branch) instead of `feature.md` and the full
plan. After approval, QA writes the failing regression test and records the run in
`qa-result.md` § Reproduction, and the Technical Lead commits it alone (`WIP(<slug>): failing test`).
Full-stack then writes `progress.md` § Root cause (cause, since when, why no test caught it, the same
pattern elsewhere) before making the smallest fix, without editing the test, and stops if the fix
crosses the plan's fix limit. QA re-runs on the plan's ACs with the test unchanged. There is no
`retro.md`: the learnings file, with its **Escaped** line, is the look-back and counts toward the
prune. A hotfix is bug mode with **Branch:** hotfix, approved by the human with the plan, and the
rest of `harness/RELEASE.md` § Hotfix unchanged.

## Why

A bug went through full mode, which asks for a feature brief and a plan that designs the fix before
anyone knows the cause, or through light mode, which has no QA and no record. Neither made sure the
bug was reproduced by a test before the fix, so a regression test could pass with and without the
fix, and the cause and how it escaped were never written down for the prune to see.

## How to apply

1. Add `harness/templates/bug-plan.md` as it is.
2. Merge § Bug mode and the two "Choosing mode" rows into `harness/MODES.md`; § Root cause into
   `harness/templates/progress.md` and § Reproduction into `harness/templates/qa-result.md`, each
   above the section they precede in the kit.
3. Merge § Bug mode and the ownership wording into `harness/agents/fullstack-engineer.md` and
   `harness/agents/qa-engineer.md`; the exception line, the template line, the flow line and the two
   new prompt blocks into `harness/HANDOFFS.md`.
4. Merge the small wording changes: the Bug row and retro sentence in `harness/SETUP.md`, the folder
   comments in `harness/STRATEGY.md`, the opening of § Hotfix in `harness/RELEASE.md`, the MODES row
   and template list in `harness/README.md`, the bug-mode bullet and command row in
   `.claude/rules/technical-lead.md`, the bullet in `.claude/rules/harness.md`, the bug-mode sentence
   in `.claude/rules/testing.md`, and the MODES row in `AGENTS.md`.
5. When `project/agents/qa-engineer.md` limits which files QA may touch, add the regression test
   file of a bug-mode plan as the one exception.

## Skip if

The project wants every bug planned as a full feature, or keeps fixing bugs in light mode.
