## What changed

- When `bash scripts/risk-check.sh` prints `Mode: plain`, the main chat makes the change itself: light mode and small mode run no Full-stack or QA subagent. New `harness/MODES.md` § Working alone says what it follows instead: the builder's rules (`harness/agents/fullstack-engineer.md` § Do, § UI screenshots, § Before handoff, and `project/agents/fullstack-engineer.md`), the `before_qa` checks, for small mode the `before_signoff` checks on the final commit, and the risk check again before the hand-off.
- Small mode keeps its plan and the human's approval. The plan template drops § Agent effort and gains § Check, filled on the final commit in place of `progress.md` and `qa-result.md`: each check with its exit code, and each criterion with the test or output that shows it.
- The Full-stack light-mode brief in `harness/HANDOFFS.md` is gone. The Technical Lead's "no application code, no QA" limits now say they are for full and bug mode.
- Full mode, bug mode and research mode are unchanged.

## Why

On everyday changes the hand-offs between agents cost more than the work: each agent re-reads the plan, the rules and the code. The risk check already sends risky changes to full mode, where the three roles still split.

## How to apply

1. Merge `harness/MODES.md` (the new § Working alone, § Light mode's process, § Small mode's process and § Choosing mode step 3), `harness/templates/small-plan.md`, `harness/HANDOFFS.md` (the ownership line, and remove the light-mode brief), `harness/SETUP.md` (the modes table and the `medium` line), `harness/TECHNICAL_LEAD.md`, `AGENTS.md` and `.claude/rules/harness.md`.
2. A small plan already in progress keeps its agents: finish it as it was planned. New small plans use the template.
3. If a retro added a rule that sends light or small changes to Full-stack or QA, bring it to the owner: keep it only as a reason to raise a change to full mode.

## Skip if

You want every change, however small, built and checked by separate agents. The risk check's `--force full` or `mode.force: full` gives that without skipping this update.
