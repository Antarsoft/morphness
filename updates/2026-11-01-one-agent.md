## What changed

- When `bash scripts/risk-check.sh` prints `Mode: plain`, the main chat makes the change itself: light mode and small mode run no Full-stack or QA subagent. New `harness/MODES.md` § Working alone says what it follows instead. It reads the builder's rules (`harness/agents/fullstack-engineer.md` § Do, § UI screenshots, § Before handoff, and `project/agents/fullstack-engineer.md`) and, for a UI change or one that needs live services, QA's layout-pass and local-stack rules. It runs the `before_qa` checks and, in small mode, the `before_signoff` checks on the final commit. It runs the risk check again before the hand-off.
- Small mode keeps its plan and the human's approval. The plan template drops § Agent effort and gains § Check, filled on the final commit in place of `progress.md` and `qa-result.md`. § Check records the commit, each check with its exit code, and each criterion with the test or output that shows it. The merge gate (`harness/CONTROLS.md`, `harness/TECHNICAL_LEAD.md`, `harness/SETUP.md` § After QA pass, the PR template) accepts § Check as the record for small mode.
- Light mode stops and becomes small mode when the change turns out to alter behaviour or to need a test of its own.
- The Technical Lead's "no application code, no QA, always delegate" rules now say they are for full and bug mode. This covers `AGENTS.md`, `.claude/rules/harness.md`, `harness/TECHNICAL_LEAD.md`, `harness/HANDOFFS.md` § Mandatory flow, `harness/STRATEGY.md` and the Claude Projects playbook. The Full-stack light-mode brief is gone.
- An owner who forces plain (`--force plain` or `mode.force: plain`) on a change to a risk path now gets one agent and no QA agent. Before, Full-stack and QA ran at `high`.
- `scripts/evidence.sh` leaves small-mode features out of the QA first-time and QA-rounds figures. The learnings template writes `QA rounds: none (small mode)` for them.
- Full mode, bug mode and research mode are unchanged.

## Why

On everyday changes the hand-offs between agents cost more than the work: each agent re-reads the plan, the rules and the code. The risk check already sends risky changes to full mode, where the three roles still split.

## How to apply

1. Merge `harness/MODES.md`: the new § Working alone, § Light mode's process and stop line, § Small mode's process, and § Choosing mode step 3. Then merge `harness/templates/small-plan.md`, `harness/templates/learnings-entry.md` and `harness/templates/steps.md`.
2. Merge the scoping lines into `AGENTS.md`, `.claude/rules/harness.md`, `harness/TECHNICAL_LEAD.md`, `harness/HANDOFFS.md` and `harness/STRATEGY.md`. In `HANDOFFS.md`, remove the light-mode brief. Then merge `harness/CONTROLS.md` § Merge gate, `harness/SETUP.md`, `.github/pull_request_template.md`, `harness/agents/fullstack-engineer.md`, `project/HARNESS_AGENTS.md` and `scripts/evidence.sh`.
3. With the `claude-projects` module, merge its README, then paste the updated playbook into the Project's custom instructions again. Every thread reads that copy first, and the old one tells the Technical Lead never to write code.
4. Look for small-mode rules in `project/` (`project/HARNESS_AGENTS.md`, `project/agents/*.md`, any process file a retro wrote) that set `medium` effort for small mode or send small changes to Full-stack or QA. Bring each one to the owner, and keep it only as a reason to raise a change to full mode.
5. A small plan already in progress keeps its agents: finish it as planned. New small plans use the template.

## Skip if

You want every change, however small, built and checked by separate agents. The risk check's `--force full` or `mode.force: full` gives that without skipping this update.
