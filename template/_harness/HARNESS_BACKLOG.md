# Harness improvement backlog

Larger harness changes that don't fit a retro action item (a reorganisation, a new module, a change across many files), found by a harness review, a prune or the owner. Each lands as its own light-mode docs change, or as a feature when it touches code, approved by the owner item by item. Tick items off here, or remove them, as they land; a prune may drop items that no longer apply.

## Item format

```markdown
### <N>. <Short title> (high | medium | low)

- **Status:** open | approved | in progress | done in <feature or change>
- **Problem:** what goes wrong today, with evidence (a retro, a prune audit, a count).
- **Change:** what to do.
- **Files:** the files it touches.
```
