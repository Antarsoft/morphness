# Brief: <product name>

Written by the owner before `/morph-auto start`. The director turns it into `docs/product/`; keep it short and say what matters to you. Anything you leave out, the director decides and lists in the product doc for your approval.

## What it is

One paragraph: the product, who it is for, and why they would use it.

## What the first version must do

The features or milestones that must work, in the order you care about them. Say what is out of scope.

## Platform and stack

Where it runs (web, iOS, Android, desktop, server) and the stack if you have one in mind. The repo's own stack wins if there is one.

## Look and feel

References, tone, art direction, placeholder art or final art.

## Money

How it makes money, if it does, and any limits on spend (hosting, services). Prices are yours to set: the director drafts assumptions only.

## Constraints

Deadlines, accounts and services you already have, things only you will do (store submits, payments setup, legal pages), and anything the run must never do.

## Done means

How you will judge the first version: the checks or demos that tell you it is finished.
