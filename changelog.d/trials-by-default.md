A retro item that changes how a role works (an agent file, a role's model or effort, a rule a role follows) is now approved as a trial by default: the rule lands tagged `(trial: <slug>)` with a trial file and Baseline, runs its length, and is decided at the prune only after the escape window of one prune window has closed. Project facts, mechanisms and owner-marked permanent items skip the trial. The retro template's Decision gains `approve (trial)` and `approve (permanent, why)`.
