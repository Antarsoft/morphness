# Code of Conduct

Morphness follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and keep lesson reviews
about the evidence. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing someone's private information are
not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to a maintainer ([@ziadantar90](https://github.com/ziadantar90)) rather
than in a public issue. Reports are handled in confidence, and maintainers may remove comments,
close threads or block accounts that break this code.
